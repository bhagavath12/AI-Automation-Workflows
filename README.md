# AI Automation Workflows

Welcome to the AI Automation Workflows repository. This repository contains a comprehensive collection of n8n automation blueprints designed to streamline marketing, sales, human resources, and customer support using AI and third-party integrations.

All workflows have been sanitized to remove sensitive API keys and credentials.

## 📂 Root Workflows

Below is a brief explanation of each workflow in the main directory:

- **Gemini File Search:** Uses Google Gemini AI to intelligently search, parse, and retrieve information from uploaded files and documents.
- **Hr Shortlisting workflow:** Automates the recruitment process by scoring and shortlisting candidate profiles or resumes based on predefined criteria.
- **Linkedin Content Generator:** Automatically generates tailored content ideas and drafts for LinkedIn posts using AI.
- **Linkedin posting:** Automates the publishing and scheduling of content directly to a LinkedIn profile or company page.
- **Linkedin reserch agent:** An autonomous AI agent that gathers research, insights, and data from LinkedIn profiles and topics.
- **Onboarding Workflow:** Streamlines the onboarding process for new clients or employees by automating emails, tasks, and system setups.
- **Personalization rateing bot:** An AI tool that evaluates outbound outreach messages and scores them based on their level of personalization.
- **Proposal generator:** Automatically compiles and generates customized business proposals based on lead data and parameters.
- **RAG agent:** A Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) bot that answers questions by referencing a specific set of knowledge base documents.
- **UGC Ads Veo & Sora (1):** Uses generative AI models (like Sora and Veo) to create scripts and prompts for User-Generated Content (UGC) video ads.
- **Vector database(rag knowledge base):** Manages the ingestion and querying of document embeddings into a vector database for RAG applications.
- **analytics:** Collects, aggregates, and processes analytical data across different platforms to generate insights.
- **campeign:** A general workflow for managing, scheduling, and executing marketing outreach campaigns.
- **email personalisation:** Uses AI to craft highly personalized cold emails by analyzing prospect data and context.
- **lead to meeting:** Automates the sales funnel from the moment a lead is captured to successfully booking a meeting on the calendar.
- **linedin connections:** Automates the process of sending LinkedIn connection requests and follow-up sequences.
- **manychat:** Integrates with Manychat to automate Instagram or Facebook direct message conversational flows.
- **marketing:** Generates marketing copy, creative direction, and video prompts tailored to specific target audiences and brand voices.
- **porfolio:** Automates the updating or management of portfolio assets and digital showcases.
- **ready to use digital assets (1):** Processes raw images using AI visual analysis to generate luxury branding assets, ad scripts, and structured data.
- **vapi:** Integrates with Vapi.ai voice agents to log call outcomes (like appointments) into Google Sheets and trigger confirmation emails.
- **whatsapp agent:** An autonomous AI agent designed to interact with users over WhatsApp.
- **whatsapp template:** Manages the generation and dispatch of pre-approved template messages over WhatsApp.
- **zoho cliq:** A custom AI bot integrated into Zoho Cliq that answers internal team queries by searching through specific company documents (like AWS Terms).

## 📂 WhatsApp Automation Folder

This dedicated folder contains advanced sequences and bots specifically for WhatsApp marketing and support:

- **whatsapp chatbot:** A fully interactive, memory-enabled AI chatbot that answers incoming WhatsApp messages, understands intent, and logs user data into Google Sheets.
- **Daily campeign:** A scheduled automation that runs daily to send marketing broadcasts and campaign updates to a targeted list of WhatsApp contacts.
- **whatsapp bulk sending:** A utility workflow designed for broadcasting announcements, newsletters, or promotional offers to a large list of WhatsApp numbers simultaneously.
- **post-call followup sequence (1):** Triggers an automated sequence of WhatsApp messages to nurture a lead immediately following a sales call, prompting them to take the next steps.
